# Handover — v0.3C Procurement

## Baseline

v0.3C extends:

`develop @ c07b4a5`

The implementation reuses the merged v0.3B generic approval foundation and
inventory source-reference / valuation contracts.

## Purchase orders

Positive-value purchase orders require a second approver under the default
policy because the default approval threshold is zero.

Organizations may deliberately create a policy version with a higher threshold.

Approved/submitted PO lines are frozen.

## Goods receipts

Goods receipts reference approved PO lines and an inventory location.

Within-tolerance receipts post inventory immediately inside the same business
transaction.

Over-tolerance receipts retain evidence but do not create stock until exception
approval.

The physical receipt timestamp is backdate-authorized when the receiver records
the document. If approval happens later, the stored authorized timestamp is used
for the inventory movement rather than reclassifying approval delay as a
backdate attempt.

## Inventory valuation boundary

Receipt inventory uses the approved PO base-unit price snapshot.

Supplier invoice price differences remain procurement evidence for the later
Accounting domain. v0.3C does not rewrite historical inventory cost.

## Currency boundary

Purchase orders and supplier invoices are restricted to the organization's
configured currency until the future FX/accounting layer is available.

## Supplier invoices

Duplicate protection normalizes supplier invoice numbers by removing
non-alphanumeric separators and uppercasing before applying the organization +
supplier unique key.

Procurement records tax amount as evidence only. Tax treatment is deferred.

## Three-way match

Each match creates a new immutable run.

The engine checks:

- received against ordered upper tolerance;
- cumulative invoiced against received upper tolerance;
- invoice price against ordered price;
- invoice amount against expected amount;
- PO and invoice currency.

An unlinked invoice line is an explicit exception.

## Payment boundary

`matched`, `within_tolerance`, and `exception_approved` mean procurement
eligibility only.

No procurement endpoint marks an invoice paid.

## Hardening

Repository SQL adds privileged triggers for:

- submitted purchase-order lines;
- goods-receipt lines;
- supplier-invoice lines;
- match runs;
- match lines.

Apply and verify these on development and test only after CI is green.
