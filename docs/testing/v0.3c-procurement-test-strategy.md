# v0.3C Procurement Test Strategy

The increment must prove:

- PO create idempotency;
- maker cannot approve own threshold-controlled PO;
- submitted PO lines cannot be changed through the model;
- partial receipts update both inventory and PO state;
- receipt movement contains procurement source reference;
- over-receipt does not post inventory before approval;
- receiver cannot approve their own receiving exception;
- goods receipt correction uses owning reversal workflow;
- raw generic reversal cannot bypass receipt workflow;
- normalized duplicate supplier invoice numbers are rejected;
- exact three-way match becomes payment-eligible;
- price variance creates an exception;
- matcher cannot approve own exception;
- cumulative split invoices cannot exceed received stock silently;
- procurement policy creates a new effective version;
- low-value PO can auto-approve when policy explicitly permits;
- goods-receipt and supplier-invoice evidence lines are immutable;
- existing identity, commercial, inventory, audit, and frontend regressions stay
  green.

After CI, privileged database testing must independently prove direct SQL
tampering is rejected for frozen PO lines and append-only procurement evidence.
