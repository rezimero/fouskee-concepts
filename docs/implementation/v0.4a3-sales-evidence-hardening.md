# v0.4A3 — Sales Evidence Hardening

This slice installs and verifies the database anti-tamper boundary for the
Sales order and quotation aggregates.

Protected evidence:
- Sales order commercial header fields after draft;
- Sales order lines after draft;
- quotation commercial header fields after draft;
- quotation lines after draft;
- reopening non-draft documents to draft;
- deletion of non-draft Sales orders and quotations.

Operational controls:
- triggers are installed in development and test by a root-controlled script;
- runtime `fouskee_app` grants are checked before hardening and never widened;
- the verifier confirms all 10 exact trigger names in both schemas;
- trigger definers must not be `fouskee_app`;
- trigger bodies must contain explicit SQLSTATE tamper signals;
- existing Sales and complete Laravel/MySQL regressions run after installation.

No schema migration is required for this slice.
No inventory reservation, fulfillment, payment, or POS behavior is added.

Next: v0.4B1 binds approved Sales orders to controlled inventory reservations
at an explicitly selected active fulfillment location.
