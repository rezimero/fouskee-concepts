# v0.4A2 — Sales Quotation Foundation

Included:
- persistent quotations and quotation lines;
- server-authoritative customer price-list resolution;
- commercial snapshots;
- validity dates;
- draft -> sent -> accepted -> converted lifecycle;
- application-level immutability after send;
- organization scoping and audit events;
- idempotent quotation creation;
- idempotent one-to-one conversion to a draft Sales order;
- preservation of price-exception evidence into the order;
- focused MySQL regression coverage.

Not included:
- customer portal acceptance;
- quotation PDF/email rendering;
- inventory reservations;
- final tax/invoice posting;
- payment allocation;
- DB trigger hardening for Sales evidence.

The next backend slice will establish Sales evidence hardening and order-stock
reservation contracts before POS/cashier work begins.
